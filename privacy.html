Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 September 2026

This policy describes how Boing intends to handle personal information in connection with the Boing app and website. It should be reviewed and approved by the company’s legal adviser before launch.

Information we collect

We may collect information you provide when you create an account or contact us, including your name, phone number, and contact details. When you use payment and account-linking features, we may process information needed to provide those services, such as payment details, account identifiers, transaction records, and authorisation information.

How we use information

We use information to provide and secure Boing, process requested payments, maintain account and transaction records, respond to support requests, prevent misuse, and improve our services. We do not sell personal information.

Banking information

When you connect a bank account, authorisation is handled through the relevant open-banking provider. Boing only accesses information and permissions you authorise, and uses it for the purpose described when you give consent.

Sharing information

We may share information with service providers that help us operate Boing, with financial institutions or open-banking providers when needed to complete a requested service, or when required by law. We expect service providers to protect information and use it only for the agreed purpose.

Security and retention

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards to protect personal information. We retain information only for as long as needed for the purposes described here, to meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

Your choices and questions

You can contact us to ask about personal information we hold about you, request a correction, or raise a privacy concern. Email danny.cash@boing.nz. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Boing develops. The latest version will be posted on this page with its updated date.
