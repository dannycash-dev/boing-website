Pricing

Our goal is for Boing transactions to be free for everyone, whether you are sending money for yourself or accepting it as a merchant.

A free-to-use foundation

We do not want fees to get in the way of useful, everyday payments. Making transactions free is the starting point we are working toward for both personal and merchant use.

Building and operating a reliable service still needs funding. We are exploring ways to support Boing without quietly putting a charge on every transaction.

Help shape how Boing is funded

We are open to users influencing the model. We could run a Polis-style process, or another user-led approach, to understand what people think is fair and sustainable.

  • A voluntary app subscription for people who want to support Boing
  • A small transaction fee, if users prefer that over a subscription
  • Another idea proposed and supported by the people who use Boing

These are possibilities, not final decisions. We will share the options clearly and listen before settling on a pricing model.
