Complaints Policy

Version 1.4 - 15 September 2026

Status: Draft for legal review

1. Have an issue and need help?

Let us know and we'll do our best to fix your problem. Making a complaint to Boing is free.

2. How to make a complaint

You can make a complaint by:

  • Emailing us at complaints@boing.nz
  • Filling out our contact form at boing.nz/contact

Please include your name, any relevant transaction details (such as the date, amount, and the phone number or BoingID involved), and a description of the issue.

3. What happens next?

Once we receive your complaint, we will acknowledge it, then conduct a review. We will respond with the results of our review within [10] working days and keep you informed if the review needs more time to complete.

If your complaint relates to a payment made through open banking, we may need to contact your bank or the recipient's bank as part of our review, and we will let you know if that's the case.

5. Complaints about privacy

If your complaint is specifically about how we've handled your personal information, you can also contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner, PO Box 10-094, The Terrace, Wellington 6143, phone 0800 803 909, privacy.org.nz. See our Privacy Policy for more detail on your privacy rights.

6. Complaints about our open banking accreditation
