About

Open banking should be easy. Your financial data belongs in your hands, and using it should feel clear, useful, and entirely up to you.

Make it simple

Money is already complicated enough. Boing is being built to make open banking easier to understand and easier to use, without burying the important details in jargon or friction.

Your data, your say

Your financial information is yours. You should know what is being shared, why it is needed, and who it is shared with. You stay in control of the permissions you give.

Trust is part of the product

We want Boing to be straightforward about how it works, careful with the information it handles, and honest about the choices available to you. Better financial tools start with better understanding.

We are building Boing for a future where moving and using your money feels less like a maze and more like something you can confidently own.
