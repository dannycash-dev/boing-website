3. The Boing app - What does it do?

Boing is a payment initiation and account visibility service that communicates with your New Zealand bank accounts through legislated banking APIs and enables you to:

  • Initiate a payment consent: When you initiate a payment through Boing you give us permission to send instructions to your selected bank that creates a payment consent. You will be redirected to your selected bank's secure application to prove your identity and approve the payment consent.
  • If you have a standing, enduring consent, this step may be unnecessary and your payment consent may be processed automatically by your bank.
  • You need to ensure sufficient funds are available to meet each payment consent.
  • Receive a payment request: You agree to receive notifications of payment requests in the app. You are under no obligation to action these payment requests and can decline (notify the requestor) or dismiss (no notification) them at any time.
  • Access account activity: Linked bank accounts will be securely visible through the Boing app and we will maintain account activity initiated through Boing for a period of time but not indefinitely. Full account transaction history remains the responsibility of your banking provider.
  • Save payee bank account numbers securely in the app, then recall them by mobile phone number or unique BoingID.
  • Enable and utilise other associated functionality as it is released. You will be notified in the app when new functions are available.

Boing does not move your money through our system or hold your funds. Boing is free to use for standard person-to-person payments up to a monthly limit. Some optional features (for example, faster transactions, higher-value transfers, or a business receiving tier) may carry a fee - current pricing is always available at boing.nz/pricing.

4. Your data and BoingID

To receive payment notifications or payment requests, your phone number and unique BoingID must be registered in the app at sign-up. This lets someone else's Boing app resolve your phone number or BoingID to your account so they can pay you.

  • A BoingID is a free, memorable identifier you can set along with your phone number, for people who would rather not share their phone number or want an easy way to share an identifier to receive a payment.
  • You can choose which of your linked bank accounts a payment made to your phone number or BoingID is paid into, and you can change this at any time.
  • You can deactivate or remove your phone number or BoingID from the directory at any time in the app. Once removed, no new payments can be sent to that identifier until you re-register it.
  • If you stop using the mobile number linked to your Boing account (for example, if you change providers or the number is reassigned by your provider), you must update or remove it from Boing as soon as possible. We may also periodically ask you to reconfirm a phone number that has been inactive, to reduce the risk of a payment being sent to a number that no longer belongs to you.
  • We show the payer a name check before a payment is sent, so they can confirm they're paying the right person. You're responsible for checking that the name and details shown for your own account are correct.

Boing follows our privacy policy when handling your information. By using our service, you agree to provide accurate information when registering and will maintain that information as current while using the service.
